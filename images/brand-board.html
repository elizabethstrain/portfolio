Who We Are 04
"Design rooted in classical principles, responsive to place, and built to endure."
Founded in Atlanta in 1991, Harrison Design is one of the nation's premier full-service design firms in the United States. With six offices across the country, the firm combines the depth of a national practice with the attentiveness of a studio deeply rooted in each region's heritage. A comprehensive multi-disciplinary practice, Harrison Design offers architecture, interior design, and landscape architecture under one roof.
Disciplines & Practice 05
Architecture
High-end residential design is the core of the practice. Rooted in classical principles, each residence responds to its surroundings and client traditions.
Interiors
Our interior designers create custom environments that seamlessly integrate architectural features, custom furniture, and art curation.
Landscape
Site-sensitive landscape architecture uniting structure with terrain, celebrating native flora and timeless outdoor rooms.
Tone & Voice 06
